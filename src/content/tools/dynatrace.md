## What is Dynatrace APM?

Dynatrace is a software intelligence platform that provides observability, AI, automation, and application security. It aims to help organizations understand and manage their IT environments and applications. The platform processes data to enable decision-making and automated actions.

## Why use it in the API Lifecycle?

* **AI Observability**: Observability for Generative AI applications, LLMs, and agents.
* **Application Observability**: APM, distributed tracing, and profiling for cloud-native and enterprise stacks.
* **Application Security**: Discover, prioritize, and shield from vulnerabilities in real-time.
* **Business Observability**: Real-time business decision-making with customizable analytics.
* **Digital Experience**: Provides real-user and synthetic monitoring, and session replays.
* **Infrastructure Observability**: End-to-end infrastructure observability for multi-cloud environments.

## Best Practices

* Leverage deterministic insights to take action in real-time.
* Unify and contextually analyze data in a data lakehouse.
* Empower teams to resolve issues faster and reduce security risks using built-in and third-party agents.
* Utilize flexible and scalable subscription models to manage costs.
